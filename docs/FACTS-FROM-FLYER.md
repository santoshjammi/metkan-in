## Compliance flags (must be resolved by the owner, not by the site)

| # | Flag | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Overseas placement advertising** | India regulates overseas recruitment advertising and recruiting-agent licensing (Emigration Act 1983; RA licensing). The flyer advertises overseas placement and visa support. The site may describe MITT's stated service, but must not state or imply a licence, authorisation or government approval. Owner must confirm what may be published and whether an RA licence exists. |
| C2 | **"Visa processing and related formalities"** | Advertising visa processing is the most legally sensitive line in the flyer. Publish only as coordination through named travel-agency partners, never as a guarantee or as MITT processing visas itself. |
| C3 | **"100,000+ candidates placed overseas"** | A large attributed figure. Publish with attribution and with F14 beside it. If MITT has a documented basis (ledger, deployment records), publishing it plainly is stronger — ask. |
| C4 | **"One of the oldest"** | Superlative. Keep as attributed phrasing ("MITT states that it is…"), not as our assertion. |
| C5 | **Instructor qualifications** | Flyer says "qualified and experienced instructors". No names besides K. V. Srikanth are published. Do not invent a team roster. |
| C6 | **Photographs** | The 17 extracted images are genuine facility photographs but low resolution (363–1600px). Publish at native size only, in a documentary context, never upscaled to full width. No identifiable people appear, so no model releases are needed. |
